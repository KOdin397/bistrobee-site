Bistrobee

Bistrobee | Dardilly

It starts buzzing at lunchtime.

On the doorstep of Lyon, Bistrobee was born from a simple idea: a table where everyone finds their place, like in a beehive. People come for the food and come back for the atmosphere.

In the kitchen, we follow the seasons. Fresh, local produce, reinvented bistro classics and a board that changes every lunchtime, depending on the market.

In the dining room, teams from nearby offices take a real break, business guests close deals between the main course and coffee, and families settle on the terrace overlooking the garden while the children run off to the play area. Welcome to the hive.
