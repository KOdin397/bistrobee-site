Charging

A useful break on the road

The 16 Tesla Superchargers are in the car park of the B&B Hotel Lyon Nord, right next to the A6 motorway. Plug in your car, cross the car park and you are at the table or at the bar.

Air-conditioned in summer, heated in winter, a garden terrace on sunny days: the perfect place to spend some time while your battery fills up.

At the bar

Coffee and a treat

A hot drink, coffee, tea or hot chocolate, with a homemade sweet treat. Enjoy it on the bar sofas or on the terrace while you charge.

Coffee and a treat · €5.50

Hungry on the road?

Pizzas and snacks

For a quick bite, our team serves pizzas baked on site and a selection of snacks, at the bar or on the terrace.

And for a proper meal, the seasonal menu and the set menu of the day are waiting for you in the restaurant, lunchtime and evening.

Easy work

A private room to work in

Working in your car is far from ideal. Book one of our small private rooms for half a day or a full day: large desk, high-speed wifi, free coffee and tea.

Perfect for a meeting or an interview, for up to 4 people.

€20 per person · half day
